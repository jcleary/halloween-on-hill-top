31st October 1970 - ‘Iconic Big Top is no more…’

A dreadful tragedy unfolded in the leafy suburb of Cheadle Hulme, Stockport on Halloween this year. The world famous Hill Top Circus, which has been delighting families for the past 30 years, caught fire during its evening show. It is still unclear how the fire was started. The clowns were performing at the time of the blaze. Miraculously the audience and circus troupe managed to escape, but the clowns were not so fortunate. All the clowns sadly perished. A few days later, the clowns were buried in the graveyard adjacent to the circus. Since then, there have been sightings of ‘ghost clowns’ wandering the graveyard after dark….

We wanted to thank you all so much for all your support, and all your donations, over the past few years. We’ve done six spooky tours (and three spine-chilling graveyard!) since 2016.
